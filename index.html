Purpose

0.9.71 preserves the v0.9.70 reproductive and mortality mechanics and adds individual-level encounter timing, frequency, intervals, contexts, and outcome traces. It traces each generation and birth cohort from birth/initialization through childhood, adulthood, relationship eligibility and opportunity, relationship formation, sexual-life activation, encounters, evaluations, pregnancy-capable exposure, conception, continued pregnancy, and parenthood. No fertility tuning, relationship tuning, population target, replacement-rate feedback, rescue, or demographic steering is used.

v0.9.74 — Shared reproductive chronology prototype

Full-term reference: approximately 266 days after conception (38 weeks), equivalent to 280 days (40 weeks) from the last menstrual period. Sexual-life evaluations now receive dated queue positions, conception inherits the evaluated encounter date, and pregnancy outcomes and deliveries resolve on dated queue positions. This is a hybrid chronology build: sexual-life ticks, conception, pregnancy outcomes, and deliveries use dated queue positions, while most other lifecycle events remain annual. Non-reproductive lifecycle events still have annual granularity; individual encounters within one sexual-life tick are still grouped and require a later per-encounter event migration. It does not claim daily-resolution world simulation. No fertility probabilities are tuned.
