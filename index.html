Block 8 Prototype 0.9.21 — Family Formation Lifecycle Repair & Audit
FAMILY LIFECYCLE REPAIR · MUTABLE PREFERENCES · NO POPULATION STEERING

Purpose

0.9.21 repairs the family-formation lifecycle isolated by the 0.9.20 audit. Family preference is now a mutable Current Circumstance state rather than an immutable trait: an NPC can reconsider wanting children throughout life, whether single or partnered, while prior preferences remain part of biography. Couples receive recurring family reviews instead of one terminal review at relationship formation. Each review distinguishes YES, NOT NOW, and NO without averaging two people into a synthetic couple preference. Financial, stability, timing, existing children, and both partners’ current preferences shape the opportunity; a delay is not a lifetime veto. This repair never consults Bellwether population size, replacement rate, or a target population.
