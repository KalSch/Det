OPPORTUNITIES ARE NOT INEVITABILITIES · RECURRENCE DOES NOT RESET THE PERSON · NO POPULATION STEERING

Purpose

This build changes the decision architecture, not the population target. Opportunities are not inevitabilities. Recurrence reopens an opportunity; it does not reset the person. Relationship and parenthood intent now persist across opportunities, while material life circumstances can change readiness. Randomness remains in encounters, compatibility, conception, and events, but a fresh opportunity no longer acts as a fresh independent roll toward an inevitable life outcome. Bellwether population size and replacement rate are never consulted.
