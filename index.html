Purpose

0.9.30 addresses the two structural failures exposed by 0.9.29. Dependent children no longer make independent migration decisions: they move with their custodial household until adulthood, after which migration opportunities belong to them. Bellwether is also embedded in a persistent low-resolution external population, allowing genuine first-time immigration by already-existing outsiders. Migration is caused by stored life circumstances, never by Bellwether population size and never by a fabricated explanation. Fertility, mortality, and relationship probabilities remain unchanged for this test.
