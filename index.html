Independent engines · shared opportunities

Businesses independently respond to market conditions, while new firms may emerge from demand, gaps in capacity, local opportunity, and uncertainty. Growth, contraction, formation, and closure are possible, never guaranteed. These are provisional simulation indicators, not historical financial records. No Life Engine dependency. Supplier and customer links persist. This version revises operating returns and market crowding, and makes the pool of potential founders respond to local opportunities. No business-count target or survival rescue exists.
