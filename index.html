Purpose

0.9.62 locks in the distinction between an encounter evaluation and a pregnancy pathway. Every sexual encounter can be evaluated in context, but that evaluation does not imply pregnancy intent, pregnancy-capable exposure, or an attempted conception. Biography and current circumstance shape sexual behavior and reproductive management; only encounters that actually produce pregnancy-capable exposure proceed to biological conception resolution. No population target, replacement-rate feedback, rescue, or demographic steering is used.
