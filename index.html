Purpose

0.9.28 replaces the occasional relationship gateway with a daily relationship-state law. Every adult life is evaluated through START / STAY / END possibilities derived from that NPC’s history and current circumstances. Established relationships strongly favor STAY; stability, shared history, children, finances, relationship duration, and individual relationship drive alter how heavily the dice lean. Single adults receive continuing opportunities to encounter a viable partner rather than waiting years for a one-shot event. Relationship progression and parenthood remain separate. Intimacy, contraception, conception biology, mortality, and the prohibition on population targets/rescue remain unchanged. Daily decisions are analytically batched by year for performance while preserving the daily probability law.
