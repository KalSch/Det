Purpose

0.9.24 repairs the lifecycle failures exposed by 0.9.23 without steering Bellwether toward a population result. The engine creates relationship opportunities; NPCs decide from biography, personality, attraction, compatibility, current circumstance, prior relationship history, finances, family pressure, migration, and other life context. Adolescent dating can begin before legal adulthood and may continue across age 18. Adult dating and marriage remain possible until death. Relationships can deepen, remain informal, end for life-driven reasons, and later reform with the same or different people. A relationship ending restores future opportunity rather than stranding the survivor. Parenthood remains orthogonal to dating, household, engagement, and marriage. No population target, replacement-rate feedback, fertility rescue, migration rescue, or demographic correction is consulted.

Locked relationship principles

Opportunity, not outcome: the engine schedules chances to meet, reconnect, deepen, separate, reconcile, or marry; NPC state decides what happens.
Adolescent dating: age-appropriate dating can occur before 18 without granting adult legal status; relationships may carry into adulthood.
Lifelong horizon: adult dating and marriage remain possible from 18 until death; there is no upper-age cutoff.
Lifecycle: dating, committed partnership, cohabitation, engagement, and marriage are distinct states. No relationship is required to follow every state.
Endings: breakup, separation, divorce, distance, incompatibility, changing feelings, infidelity, finances, family pressure, migration, and death can end relationships when biography supports it.
Repartnering: an ending returns a surviving NPC to future opportunity. Lifelong singledom remains possible, but is not a mechanical dead end.
Orthogonality: relationship, household, marriage, parenthood, and family preference remain separate states.
