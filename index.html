LIFE ANCHORS ARE CIRCUMSTANCES · PLANNED + UNPLANNED PARENTHOOD · FAMILY-PRESERVING MIGRATION

Purpose

0.9.39 compression prototype: the 0.9.37 family, demographic, relationship, age-17, and family-preserving migration mechanics remain frozen. This build changes computational representation only. Routine future events belonging to DORMANT/BACKGROUND NPCs can live in year-bucketed cold storage instead of the active priority heap. They still execute at the same canonical year and retain their deterministic event keys.

Equivalence rule: compression passes only if a baseline and compressed run from the same seed produce the same canonical fingerprint through the A/B horizon. Population, identities, parentage, relationships, residence, births, deaths, migrations, queue order inputs, and diagnostic totals are compared. Compression is not allowed to buy speed by changing lives.

0.9.37 removes the remaining idea that Life Anchors are eligibility hurdles for parenthood. Biological possibility and an existing relationship create opportunities; Life Anchors and Current Circumstance shape whether an opportunity is planned or unplanned, how the people respond, and what consequences follow. Dating couples can become parents. Existing parents can have children with later partners, producing persistent half-sibling and step-family structures without rewriting biological parentage. Existing-child count is history, not a fertility penalty. Migration now acts on the NPC's current family/household structure: dependent children remain with a living custodial household, and co-resident partners move as a household unless a separately generated adult relationship/household decision establishes a split. There is no population target, replacement target, annual fertility percentage, or demographic rescue mechanism.
